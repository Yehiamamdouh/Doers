<h2>Traditional and digital Advertising</h2>

<p>The two types of advertising are traditional and digital.</p>

<p>Traditional advertising is often more expensive than digital, but it provides more ability to reach a wider range of people. It is a method of marketing that has been around since the beginning of time. This type of advertising includes TV and radio commercials, billboards, and print ads in newspapers and magazines.</p>

<p>Digital advertising is also a form of marketing but it has only been around for about twenty years or so. The best way to describe digital advertising is using an example: if you were looking to buy a product online then you would see digital advertisements on your screens such as banner ads or pop-ups which are trying to encourage you to click on them to get more information about what they’re selling. Digital advertising can be cheaper and easier to customize, but it may not deliver as many impressions as traditional advertisements. Traditional advertising can be effective because people have grown up with it, meaning they understand the significance behind certain brands which makes them want those products even more than before.</p>
