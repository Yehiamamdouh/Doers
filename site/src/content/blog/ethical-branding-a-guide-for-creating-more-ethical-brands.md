<h2>What are some common pitfalls to avoid when creating an ethical brand?”</h2>

<p>There are a few common traps that companies can fall into when trying to create an ethical brand. By being aware of these potential pitfalls, you can avoid them and ensure your company’s values are represented authentically. Some of the most common mistakes include neglecting the customer, toeing the line between ethics and legality, and relying on marketing gimmicks instead of actual ethical practices. Avoiding these missteps is crucial to maintaining a reputable image and building consumer trust.</p>
