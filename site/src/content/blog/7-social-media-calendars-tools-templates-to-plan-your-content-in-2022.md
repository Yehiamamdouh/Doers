Before you begin to plan your content for the week or month, it is important to make sure that you have a plan in place. There are many out there but it can be difficult to choose which one will work best for your business! What works well for one brand might not necessarily work well for another.</p>

Creating a digital marketing plan or digital marketing strategy is the first step towards developing an effective digital presence for your business – digital marketing strategies are never set in stone and should be an ongoing process that is constantly evolving.</p>

<h2>ContentCal:</h2>

<p>ContentCal is a great tool for planning, organizing, and executing the content that you want to share on social media. There are calendars to help you plan your posts, tools for easy publishing, and templates so that you know what types of content will work best for each social network.</p>

<p>ContentCal has created templates based on research about which types of content perform well with each different network. It can help keep track of when your posts go live on social networks like Facebook, Twitter &amp; LinkedIn. With this information readily available at your fingertips in one convenient place, it makes it easier than ever to schedule your social media posts ahead of time.</p>

<h2>SproutSocial:</h2>

<p>The key to a successful social media campaign is consistency. Having a calendar is one of the best ways to keep your followers engaged and coming back for more. Sprout Social has created some amazing tools that can help you stay organized, allowing you to plan ahead and be consistent with your content strategy.</p>

<p>Sprout Social is a social media management tool that allows you to manage your social media accounts from one place. It lets you schedule, collaborate and analyze all your posts in one place to make sure they get the most reach possible.</p>

<h2>HubSpot:</h2>

<p>Every business needs to be on social media in order to stay relevant. But the truth is, it’s not easy to manage all of your social media accounts when you’re juggling other responsibilities like running a company and managing employees. That’s why HubSpot created this editorial content calendar template that will help keep your posts on track for Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google+, Reddit, Pinterest, and more.</p>

<p>HubSpot’s Editorial Content Calendar Template for social media management is a scheduling and content strategy tool that helps you plan all your social media posts in advance. There are many benefits of using HubSpot’s Editorial Content Calendar Template for social media management. One being it will help you save time by having everything planned out in advance so that when Monday morning comes around you won’t have to scramble to create content or schedule posts on each social network individually. Another benefit is it will help increase engagement with followers since they’re able to see what they can expect from you every day of the week through their newsfeeds.</p>

<h2>Hootsuite:</h2>

<p>Hootsuite is a social media management platform that allows teams to collaboratively execute campaigns across multiple social networks. Using Hootsuite, you can schedule updates, engage with followers and track the progress of your social strategy all in one place. Hootsuite is an online platform that allows users to monitor, post, and engage on various social media sites. With Hootsuite tools, calendars, and templates you can easily plan your content within the platform for use on multiple social networks at once.</p>

<h2>Loomly:</h2>

<p>Loomly is a social media calendar to plan, schedule, and publish content for multiple accounts at once. The tool offers advanced features like team collaboration and the ability to track your campaign’s performance. Loomly is an all-in-one platform that makes it easy to manage social media campaigns across different channels in one place. With this system, you can create posts, run contests, analyze analytics, build reports on key metrics – everything you need.</p>

<h2>Agorapulse:</h2>

<p>You don’t need an army of social media editors and marketers to plan your content. You can do it yourself with Agorapulse. Agorapulse is a social media management tool, but they also have tools to help you plan out your content for each day. Whether you need calendars or templates, Agorapulse has everything you need to make sure that your content gets posted on the days and times that best fit your business’s needs.</p>

<h2>Buffer:</h2>

<p>A buffer is a tool that allows users to schedule social media posts on Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google+, Pinterest, and Instagram and monitor analytics. With this tool, you can plan out your content for the week or month as well as post it all at once. With the help of their tools and templates for creating your own custom content calendars, you can be sure to always have something fresh scheduled for your social media accounts.</p>
