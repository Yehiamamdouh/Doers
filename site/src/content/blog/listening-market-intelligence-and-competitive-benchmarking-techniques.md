<p>Listening is the most important technique in market intelligence. You can gain valuable information by listening to conversations over social media, forums, online reviews, and more. <h2>Market Intelligence and Competitive Benchmarking Techniques:</h2>

<p>Market Intelligence is the combination of data and analytics that provides insight into what customers want or need. It involves understanding how your competitors are doing things, who their customers are, where they get their information from and how they make decisions. With this information, you can position yourself to better meet the needs of your clients while using your competitor’s weaknesses against them. This also helps you avoid costly mistakes by learning from others’ experiences instead of having to find out through trial-and-error methods.</p>

<p>The world is constantly changing and it’s important to stay ahead of the game. To do this, you need to know your competition inside and out. Market intelligence will give you an insight into what they’re currently doing so that you can better plan for the future. This way, when competitors try new things or introduce new products, you’ll be able to respond right away with a competitive strategy in place.</p>
