<h2>Difference between SEO and Social SEO:</h2>

<p>Organic search engine optimization is a process of using specific strategies to rank higher on Google and other major search engines. Social SEO, on the other hand, is a process of increasing visibility by optimizing for social media networks such as Facebook and Twitter. These two methods are not mutually exclusive but should be used in tandem to achieve the best results.</p>
