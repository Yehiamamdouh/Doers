The digitalization of businesses has led to people cutting out their reliance on physical offices and paper documents, while at the same time it is making companies more profitable. Digital technologies have already had a major influence on our economy in recent years – they’ve enabled new ways to deliver goods and services across borders; cut down barriers for entrepreneurs looking to start businesses; improved efficiency in all sectors of society; created jobs that didn’t exist before; changed the way people buy things online or at brick-and-mortar stores; helped create entirely new markets like social media networks. It’s hard to imagine what life would be like without mobile phones or laptops when it comes to keeping up with family members</p>

<p>Digital platforms are reshaping the global business sector. These days, digital platforms are not just disrupting industries – they’re shaping them.
